# Warm Academic Paper & Ink

Warm academic editorial system for MaturaKorki — a Polish marketplace for high-school maths tutoring for the matura exam. The visual language is tactile paper and ink: warm vellum backgrounds, crisp white cards, deep spruce primary actions, amber accents, and confident Fraunces headlines paired with Inter for UI and body copy.

Use the exact tokens above. Do not introduce blue-grey Material surfaces, gradients, purple, 3D objects, or extra decorative sections. Keep the paper palette warm, the borders fine, and the typography editorial.
